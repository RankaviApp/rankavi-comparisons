## Starting With What Each Product Actually Is

Outreachz runs a guest post marketplace with fixed link packages. Rankavi runs an unlinked mention engine instead. It is built for AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Both are self-serve since pricing is published. But the buying experience differs quite a bit.

## Breaking Down the Package Model

Outreachz publishes clear tiered pricing. Starter gets 5 links for $700. Performance gets 12 links for $2,000. Growth is 18 links for $3,000. Pro tops out at 30 links for $5,000. That works out to roughly $140 to $170 per link. The packages are genuinely transparent, which deserves credit.

Rankavi does not use packages at all. Each mention costs $15 on its own. It drops to $12 with a 50-credit bulk pack. There is no minimum bundle required to start.

## Do the Math Before You Buy

Outreachz's Pro tier runs roughly $167 per link. That is still well above Rankavi's $12 to $15 per mention. The products are not identical, of course. A guest post link and an AI-engine mention serve different purposes. But on raw unit economics, Rankavi is meaningfully cheaper.

## Contract Length and Renewal Pressure

Outreachz packages are one-time purchases, not recurring contracts. That is a fair structure for buyers testing first. Rankavi is also contract-free and pay-as-you-go. Neither company locks you into a retainer. Both beat much of the broader digital PR industry here.

## Getting Your First Link or Mention Live

With a package marketplace like Outreachz, timelines depend on publisher fulfillment. Larger packages can take longer to fully deliver. Rankavi generates mentions directly through its own system. That tends to produce faster, more predictable turnaround.

## How You Actually Sign Up and Buy

Outreachz publishes its pricing page openly. You see package costs before committing, no sales call needed. Rankavi works the same way. Instant self-serve access comes through the dashboard or API. Both companies compete well against sales-gated agencies here.

## What You Can See About Your Order

Outreachz's published tiers give upfront cost clarity. The specific publishers used are not always known beforehand. Rankavi's dashboard shows cost, placement, and performance per mention. That visibility applies at the individual unit level, not a bundle.

## Traditional Links Versus AI-Native Mentions

This is the real strategic fork. Outreachz sells guest post links, a classic SEO tactic. It builds domain authority and referring link diversity. Google has weighted that signal for years. It remains a legitimate strategy for organic rankings.

Rankavi exists for a different problem entirely. AI answer engines do not rank pages the traditional way. They synthesize responses from mentions across the web. Often no link is attached at all. If your growth depends on AI-driven discovery, guest post links won't solve that. Rankavi was built specifically to.

## Scaling Your Spend Over Time

Outreachz scales through bigger packages, up to the 30-link Pro tier. The per-link rate improves modestly at higher volume. Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and an API instead. Your per-mention cost can drop to $12. A developer can request more volume programmatically too.

## Where Outreachz Holds Its Own

Outreachz's transparent, published package pricing is genuinely rare. Many guest post competitors hide costs behind a sales call. If your strategy is classic link building, this matters. You get a clear, no-surprises price before buying. That consistency has real value for a fixed SEO budget.

## Comparing a Real Package to a Real Bulk Pack

Take the Outreachz Growth tier: 18 links for $3,000. That works out to about $167 per link. Spend that same $3,000 with Rankavi at $12 per mention. You get 250 mentions instead. Even accounting for different asset types, the unit economics favor Rankavi by a wide margin.

That gap does not erase what Outreachz offers, though. Guest post links carry lasting SEO value on the hosting site. A mention does not replicate that specific asset. Buyers who need a live, indexed link are paying for something Rankavi was never built to provide.

## Two Budgets, Two Different Jobs

Teams running a classic link building campaign reach for Outreachz. Its packages are structured exactly for that purpose. Teams trying to get cited inside ChatGPT reach for Rankavi instead. That is the specific mechanism its product targets. Knowing which job you're solving makes the decision clearer.

## Package Structure Versus Per-Unit Pricing

Outreachz forces a choice between four fixed package sizes. If your real need falls between tiers, you overpay slightly. A campaign needing 8 links must buy the 12-link Performance tier. Rankavi has no such tier gap to navigate. Buy exactly the number of mentions your campaign needs. There is no rounding up to the next bracket.

This matters more than it first appears for smaller budgets. A team testing a new market may want just a few placements. Outreachz's $700 minimum package can feel like overcommitment there. Rankavi's per-mention pricing lets you start smaller and scale later. That flexibility suits experimental campaigns particularly well.

## The Underlying Question to Ask Yourself

Figure out what asset actually moves your business first. A guest post link lives permanently on a publisher's domain. It passes classic link equity toward your site over time. A Rankavi mention is a citation for an AI-generated answer. No publisher relationship is needed at all. Neither replaces the other, and strong strategies use both.

Consider where your buyers start their research too. If most still type queries into Google, Outreachz remains reasonable. If more now ask ChatGPT for direct recommendations, that audience skips search results. That is precisely the gap Rankavi exists to close.

## A Note on What Fulfillment Actually Looks Like

Outreachz packages route through its publisher network for fulfillment. Each link lands on a real, established site. That process takes coordination time behind the scenes. Rankavi's mentions generate through its own internal system instead. No outside publisher relationship is needed to complete an order. That difference is a big part of the speed gap.

## The Bottom Line

If fixed guest post packages with clear pricing suit your goals, Outreachz is a fair option. If the lowest per-unit cost and AI-answer placements matter more, Rankavi is the sharper choice.
