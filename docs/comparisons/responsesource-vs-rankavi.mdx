ResponseSource is a long-running UK journalist enquiry platform. Rankavi is a newer platform built for AI-search mentions. The pricing structures alone make these two very different bets.

## Annual subscription vs pay per mention

ResponseSource's Journalist Enquiry Service starts at £625 per year, per category you want to monitor. Add-ons stack on top of that, including a 15-minute priority service at £20 and PA distribution at £70.

We're treating ResponseSource's pricing as not fully verified in our records. It is category-based, and add-ons can shift the real annual cost. The £625 starting figure is what they publish. Your actual spend will likely land higher once you add categories or services.

Rankavi's pricing has no such variables. It's $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention if you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. There are no categories, no add-ons, and no annual commitment sitting underneath it.

## What you're actually paying for

ResponseSource gives you access to journalist enquiries within your chosen categories for a full year. It's a subscription to an ongoing stream of opportunities you then have to respond to yourself.

Rankavi sells the mention directly. There's no enquiry stream to monitor and no pitch to write. It's just a mention engineered for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

Say you have a PR team that enjoys actively monitoring enquiries and responding fast. ResponseSource's model rewards that kind of ongoing effort. If you want a more direct, hands-off mechanism, Rankavi's model requires less day-to-day management.

## Getting started

ResponseSource requires an annual subscription purchase per category. That's a bigger upfront commitment than a typical self-serve signup, even without a formal sales call.

Rankavi is self-serve from the start: sign up, use the dashboard or API, and pay only for what you use.

## Commitment

ResponseSource's category subscriptions run on an annual basis. You're locked into that spend for the year regardless of how many enquiries you actually respond to.

Rankavi has no annual commitment and no contract. You buy mentions as needed and can stop whenever it makes sense for you.

## Speed to your first result

With ResponseSource, results depend on relevant enquiries appearing in your chosen category. They also depend on you responding faster than other sources, even with the optional priority add-on helping your odds.

Rankavi's mentions move through its own dashboard and API, not dependent on the timing or volume of incoming journalist enquiries.

## Reporting

ResponseSource is built primarily as an enquiry delivery service. The site does not prominently describe a placement-tracking dashboard for measuring your actual results.

Rankavi gives direct dashboard visibility into your mentions and spend. You always know what you've received for what you paid.

## Traditional PR vs AI search

ResponseSource sits firmly in traditional UK PR and journalist outreach. It's a well-established channel, particularly strong for UK-focused brands and trade press.

Rankavi is built specifically for the AI-search channel. It's a newer priority that traditional enquiry services like ResponseSource were not designed around.

## Scaling

ResponseSource scales by adding more category subscriptions. Each one carries its own £625-plus annual cost, which can add up quickly across multiple sectors.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs, dropping the per-mention price from $15 to $12. No separate subscription is needed per category or topic.

## Why we're treating this price carefully

ResponseSource does publish a starting number, £625 per year per category. That's more transparency than many PR platforms offer upfront. We want to give them credit for that.

But a starting price is not a full price. Add-ons like the £20 priority service and £70 PA distribution can push your real annual cost meaningfully higher. The exact total depends on how many categories your brand needs to monitor. Take a B2B software company covering three relevant categories, for instance. It could be looking at well over £1,875 a year before any add-ons at all.

Rankavi doesn't have that kind of variable math. Whether you buy one mention or fifty, the price per unit is exactly what's published. That's $15, or $12 in a bulk pack. There's no category multiplier to account for.

## A long track record vs a new mechanism

ResponseSource has operated in the UK PR space for a long time. That track record carries real weight with agencies and in-house teams. They already know how to work its enquiry system effectively. Familiarity has value.

Rankavi is a newer kind of platform. It addresses a problem that barely existed in its current form a few years ago. That problem is how brands get mentioned inside AI-generated answers rather than traditional journalist-written articles.

Say your team already has an established workflow around ResponseSource and strong category coverage. Switching away from it entirely may not make sense. But layering Rankavi on top, specifically for AI-search visibility, addresses a real gap. ResponseSource's traditional enquiry model was never designed to cover it.

## Currency and geography

One more practical note. ResponseSource prices in British pounds and is oriented specifically around UK journalists and UK trade press. That matters if your target market extends beyond the UK.

Rankavi prices in US dollars and isn't tied to a single country's press or journalist ecosystem. AI answer engines don't work the same way traditional regional press outreach does.

## Budgeting for the year ahead

Planning a ResponseSource budget means estimating category count. It means deciding whether priority service is worth the extra £20. It also means deciding whether PA distribution at £70 fits that particular campaign, all before the year even starts.

Planning a Rankavi budget is simpler by comparison. Decide how many mentions you want, multiply by $15, or $12 if you're buying in bulk, and that's the number. No category forecasting required.

Some teams would rather adjust spend month to month based on results. Rankavi's pay-as-you-go structure fits that rhythm better than an annual subscription locked in ahead of time.
