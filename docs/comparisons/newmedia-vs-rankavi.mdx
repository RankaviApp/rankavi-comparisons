That is worth paying for if your team is small and stretched thin across too many priorities already.

The tradeoff is control and cost clarity. Bundled services make it harder to see exactly what any single deliverable costs. The pricing usually reflects the whole engagement rather than one line item. Rankavi gives up that bundled convenience entirely in exchange for total price clarity on the one thing it does. You always know exactly what a mention costs and exactly how many you bought.
