Featured.com, the platform that grew out of Terkel. Absorbed the HARO brand, and Rankavi both help brands get mentioned online. But the mechanics, and what you're paying for, are quite different.

## Subscriptions vs pay-per-mention

Featured.com runs on monthly subscription tiers. There is a free plan, a Lite plan at $29 a month ($348 billed annually). A Pro plan at $79 a month ($948 billed annually).

Rankavi has no subscription at all. You pay $15 per mention, or $12 per mention if you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. There is no monthly fee sitting in the background whether you use it or not.

That distinction matters for budgeting. With Featured.com, you're paying for access to pitch opportunities. The returned placement volume depends on how many pitches you send and how many land. With Rankavi, you're paying directly for the mention itself.

## How you get access

Both platforms are genuinely self-serve, which is worth crediting. Featured.com lets you sign up without a sales call, even on the free tier.

Rankavi is the same: sign up, use the dashboard or API, no sales conversation required.

Neither company gatekeeps entry behind a demo request, which puts both ahead of most PR agencies in this comparison set.

## What you're actually doing day to day

Featured.com works by matching you with journalist queries you then pitch, similar in spirit to the old HARO model. You write responses, and if a journalist picks yours, you get quoted and, often, linked.

Rankavi doesn't involve pitching journalists at all. It builds unlinked brand mentions specifically engineered for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

Featured.com is fundamentally a PR tool built around human journalists. Rankavi is built around how AI systems surface and cite brands. These are adjacent goals, not identical ones.

## Commitment and contracts

Featured.com's paid tiers are billed monthly or annually. You can presumably downgrade to free, but you're still on a subscription clock either way.

Rankavi has no contract and no subscription. You buy mentions as needed and stop whenever you want.

## Speed to your first result

With Featured.com, speed depends on pitching. Being selected by a journalist, a process with real uncertainty even on the Pro tier's higher pitch allowance.

Rankavi's mentions move through a dashboard and API, without depending on a third party choosing to feature your pitch.

## Reporting and visibility

Featured.com gives you visibility into your pitches. Their status inside its own platform, which is a real strength for a subscription tool at this price point.

Rankavi likewise gives direct dashboard visibility into mentions and spend, without needing to wait on anyone else's decision.

## Traditional PR vs AI search

Featured.com sits squarely in the traditional PR and journalist-outreach tradition, just modernized into a subscription SaaS model. It is a legitimate way to build press mentions and backlinks over time.

Rankavi is purpose-built for the newer AI-search channel, optimizing for how generative engines cite. Mention brands rather than how journalists do.

## Scaling volume

Featured.com scales mainly by upgrading tiers, from 0 pitches on free up through the Pro plan's higher allowance. That allowance is capped by whatever tier you're on.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs instead. The price per mention drops from $15 to $12 once you buy 50 at a time. No subscription tier limits how far you can go.

## The free tier question

Featured.com's free plan deserves a fair mention here. Zero dollars for access to journalist queries is a genuinely low bar to entry. It lets you test whether pitching fits your workflow before spending anything.

Rankavi doesn't offer a free tier. Its $15 flat rate per mention is still a low-risk way to test a single placement. There is no need to commit to a monthly subscription you might not fully use.

Both companies are clearly trying to lower the barrier to a first try. They just do it through different mechanisms. Featured.com uses a free access tier, while Rankavi uses a low per-unit price with no recurring cost at all.

## Writing pitches vs buying outcomes

A meaningful chunk of the work on Featured.com happens on your side. You have to write compelling, timely responses to journalist queries. That skill varies a lot from person to person and team to team.

Rankavi removes that writing burden from the mention-buying process itself. There's no pitch to craft and no journalist to convince before you see a result.

Your team might have a strong writer who enjoys the back-and-forth of journalist pitching. Featured.com can then produce outsized results relative to its subscription cost. If your team would rather not staff that skill, Rankavi's more direct mechanism avoids the dependency entirely.

## Where each fits into a broader plan

Featured.com produces the kind of press mentions. Backlinks that support traditional SEO and brand credibility over time, the same currency PR teams have chased for years.

Rankavi produces mentions aimed at a channel traditional PR tools weren't built to address. That channel is the answers AI systems generate when people ask questions instead of typing a search query.

Many teams will find room for both in a broader content. Visibility strategy, since they're not really competing for the same outcome. Featured.com builds press credibility. Rankavi builds AI-search presence.

## Annual billing and hidden savings

Featured.com's annual billing option, $348 for Lite or $948 for Pro, saves a bit versus paying month to month. That is a common SaaS pattern worth noticing when comparing total yearly cost.

Rankavi doesn't have a billing cadence to optimize around at all. There's no annual discount to chase. There's no subscription in the first place, just a lower per-unit price once you buy in bulk.

If you're the type of buyer who reads the fine print on annual versus monthly pricing, Featured.com rewards that diligence. Rankavi simplifies the decision by removing the variable entirely.

## Which to choose

Featured.com is a genuinely useful, low-cost way to build press mentions. Earn journalist-driven links, especially if your team enjoys writing sharp pitch responses.

Rankavi is the better pick if you want your brand showing up inside AI-generated answers. It is priced simply per mention with no subscription commitment.

One last point worth weighing. Featured.com's legacy as the successor to HARO carries brand recognition many marketers already trust. Can shorten the internal approval process for a new tool. Rankavi is newer and less widely known yet, but its pricing transparency. Lack of contract make it an easy, low-risk platform to trial alongside whatever you're already running.
