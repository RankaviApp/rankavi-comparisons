Choosing between a traditional PR agency and a self-serve AI-search platform comes down to your budget. Demand Local runs custom digital PR campaigns for local and national brands. Rankavi sells individual brand mentions built for AI answer engines like ChatGPT and Perplexity. Here is how the two actually compare.

## What Demand Local does well

Demand Local is a real agency with account strategists, not a self-serve tool. That matters if you need a team to plan a full campaign. It also matters if you need reporters pitched and a launch coordinated around a product or news event. Agencies like this earn their fee by building relationships with journalists over time. Those relationships can land coverage a bot or automated tool simply cannot get. If your brand needs a nuanced story told to a specific reporter, that human touch has real value.

## The pricing gap you need to know about

Here is the catch. Demand Local does not publish pricing anywhere on its site. To find out what a campaign costs, you have to book a sales call and get a custom quote. That is standard in the digital PR agency world, but it makes budgeting hard before you commit. You cannot compare costs across vendors without giving up your contact information first.

Rankavi takes the opposite approach. The price is $15 per mention if you buy standalone. It drops as low as $12 per mention when you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. That number is on the website. No call required. You know exactly what you are paying before you swipe a card.

## Speed to your first placement

Agency engagements like Demand Local's typically start with a discovery phase, then strategy, then outreach. Getting your first real placement can take weeks or months depending on how fast reporters respond. That is normal for earned media, where you are pitching a human being who has their own deadlines and interests.

Rankavi is built to move fast because it is not waiting on a journalist's inbox. You buy mentions, they get placed, and you are not stuck in a queue behind other agency clients. If speed to your first result matters more than a bespoke campaign, this is a meaningful difference.

## Signing up versus signing a contract

Demand Local's access model runs through a sales call. That usually means some kind of retainer or project-based contract once you commit. Agency contracts in this space often run three to six months minimum. Demand Local does not publish its specific terms.

Rankavi has no contract at all. It is pay-as-you-go. You buy what you need, when you need it. You are never locked into a monthly retainer you have to justify to a boss or a client.

## Reporting and transparency

A good agency will send you monthly reports showing which outlets ran your story and what the estimated reach was. That is standard practice and Demand Local likely does the same, though the specifics depend on your contract.

Rankavi's transparency comes from the model itself. Every mention has a fixed, published price, so there is nothing hidden in a report you have to interpret. You see what you bought and what it cost, without waiting for a monthly recap email.

## Built for different kinds of search

This is maybe the biggest strategic difference. Demand Local, like most PR agencies, is built around traditional SEO and earned media. That means getting your brand mentioned in news articles and blogs that Google indexes and ranks.

Rankavi is built specifically for how AI answer engines work. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews do not rank pages the way classic search does. They pull from a different pool of signals. Unlinked brand mentions play a much bigger role than they used to in traditional SEO. If your growth plan depends on showing up when an AI is asked a question, Rankavi exists for that reason. Demand Local's PR work can still help here indirectly, but it was not built for this specific goal.

## Scaling up your placements

With an agency model, scaling means renegotiating your contract or adding a bigger retainer. Every additional placement usually means another conversation with your account manager.

Rankavi scales through the platform itself. The bulk 50-credit packs bring the price down to $12 per mention. You can buy through the dashboard or the API without another phone call. If you want ten mentions this month and forty next month, you just do it.

## What kind of brand fits each model

Demand Local suits a brand with an ongoing story, a launch calendar, or a local presence tied to regional press.

If you have the budget for a retainer and the patience for a multi-week ramp-up, that investment can pay off. It can bring coverage a self-serve tool cannot replicate. Local and national press still carries weight with certain audiences. A skilled agency knows which outlets to pitch for which kind of story.

Rankavi suits a brand that needs to show up now, inside the tools people use to ask and get answers. More buyers are starting their research inside ChatGPT or Perplexity instead of typing a query into Google first. If your competitors are cited by name in those answers and you are not, that gap costs you visibility. It costs you visibility every single day it stays open. Closing it does not require a discovery call. It requires an account and a budget as small as $15.

## A note on risk and predictability

One underrated difference between these two models is risk. When you sign with an agency like Demand Local without published pricing, you commit budget before knowing the final number. Campaign results in PR are never fully guaranteed, no matter how skilled the team is. A pitch can land or it can go nowhere. You often will not know which until the retainer period is already underway.

Rankavi removes most of that uncertainty from the pricing side. You know your cost per mention before you spend anything. You are never paying a retainer for a month where nothing landed. That does not mean every strategy works equally well for every brand. But the financial risk of testing it is dramatically lower than committing to an undisclosed agency rate.
