## Both chase the same goal, differently

Perrill and Rankavi both exist because AI answer engines changed how people find brands. Perrill is a GEO and AEO agency. It builds AI-search visibility strategy the way an agency traditionally builds SEO strategy, through research, execution, and reporting.

Rankavi approaches the same goal as a product, not a service. You order mentions built for AI answer engines directly, without an agency layer in between.

## Price per placement

Perrill has not published pricing for its GEO or AEO services specifically. That is common for agencies offering newer service lines, since scope tends to vary by client. To get a number, you need a sales conversation.

Rankavi's number is already public. A single mention runs $15, and buying a 50-credit bulk pack brings that down to $12 per mention. No custom scoping is required to know your cost.

## Speed to first placement

Agency engagements like Perrill's typically start with onboarding, strategy, and a research phase before any visible output appears. That groundwork can be valuable, but it takes time before you see your first result.

Rankavi skips the onboarding runway. You order a mention and it enters production immediately, aimed at AI answer engines from day one.

## Access model

Working with Perrill means a sales call to scope your engagement. There is no self-serve signup or published rate card for GEO and AEO work. That is standard agency practice.

Rankavi is self-serve from the start. Sign up, load credits, and start ordering without waiting on a proposal.

## Contract commitment

Agencies generally structure GEO and AEO work as ongoing retainers, similar to SEO retainers. Perrill has not published specific contract terms for these services.

Rankavi has no contract requirement at all. It is pay-as-you-go, so you can order one mention or fifty without committing to a monthly retainer.

## Transparency and reporting

Agencies like Perrill typically provide detailed strategic reporting as part of a GEO or AEO engagement. That can include competitive analysis and ongoing optimization recommendations. That depth is a real strength of the agency model.

Rankavi's transparency works differently. It is simpler and price-based: you know exactly what each mention costs before you order it.

## AI-search focus vs traditional SEO/PR

This is where Perrill and Rankavi actually overlap the most. Perrill markets itself specifically as a GEO and AEO agency. Its focus sits squarely on AI-search visibility rather than legacy SEO alone.

Rankavi shares that same focus, building unlinked brand mentions specifically for ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Both companies are betting on the same shift in how people search.

## Scalability

Scaling an agency engagement with Perrill usually means expanding scope or adding retainer hours. An account manager negotiates that directly with you.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and API access. Growing your mention volume is a self-serve decision, not a renegotiation.

## What an agency relationship buys you

Perrill's agency model is not simply slower or more expensive by default. It is worth saying so plainly. A good GEO agency brings strategy, competitive research, and ongoing optimization a single self-serve order cannot replicate. If your brand needs a broader content and visibility strategy, not just individual mentions, that partnership can be genuinely valuable.

Rankavi is not trying to be a strategy partner. It is a direct product for one specific job, getting your brand mentioned where AI answer engines look. Some buyers will need both, using Rankavi for volume and an agency for strategy.

## The cost of not knowing the number

The practical downside of Perrill's unpublished GEO pricing is that comparison shopping becomes harder. You cannot line up three agency quotes side by side without going through three separate sales processes. Each one takes real time.

Rankavi removes that step for at least one option in your evaluation. You already know its number, so you can weigh it against whatever a Perrill quote eventually comes back as.

## Who tends to pick which option

Companies with an existing budget line for agency retainers often lean toward a firm like Perrill. That is especially true if they want strategic support. Companies that want to test AI-search visibility quickly, without restructuring their budget, tend to start with something like Rankavi instead.

Neither path is wrong. They answer different questions. One is who manages our AI-search strategy, and the other is how do we get mentioned starting today.

## What "GEO/AEO agency" actually means

It helps to unpack what Perrill's category label really covers, since GEO and AEO are still fairly new terms. Generative engine optimization and answer engine optimization both describe efforts to shape how AI tools summarize and cite a brand. Perrill applies agency methods, research, content, and technical adjustments, to try to improve that outcome over time.

Rankavi approaches the same target from a narrower angle. Instead of adjusting a brand's broader content and technical footprint, it places direct mentions. Those are built to be picked up by AI systems. The goal overlaps, but the mechanism is different.

## A practical way to evaluate both

If you are choosing between the two, separate the question of budget certainty from the question of strategic depth. Rankavi gives you budget certainty immediately, since its price is public and fixed regardless of scope.

Perrill may offer more strategic depth once you are through its sales process. But you will not know its price, or what is included, until that call happens. Weighing those two tradeoffs against your own team's needs is the real decision here.
