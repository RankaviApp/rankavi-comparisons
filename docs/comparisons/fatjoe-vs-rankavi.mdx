FATJOE and Rankavi are both self-serve, both transparent on price, and both let you skip the sales call. The difference is what you are actually buying: backlinks for classic SEO versus mentions built for AI answer engines. Here is the full breakdown.

## FATJOE's self-serve marketplace model

FATJOE built a genuinely useful marketplace for link building. You browse guest post and niche edit options by domain rating, add them to a cart, and check out. No sales call needed. That self-serve checkout experience is one of the better ones in link building. It deserves credit for making pricing visible before purchase.

## Comparing the actual numbers

FATJOE's guest posts start around $83 for DR10+ sites. They climb as high as $7,800 or more for top-tier media placements. A DR30 link runs $138, and a DR50 link runs $386. Niche edits range from $81 at DR10+ up to $378 at DR50+. Managed packages start at $700.

Rankavi's pricing sits well below even FATJOE's entry-level tier. It is $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention in bulk. Even FATJOE's cheapest DR10+ guest post at roughly $83 costs more than five times a single Rankavi mention.

## Why the price gap exists

This is not really an apples-to-apples product comparison, and it is worth being upfront about that. FATJOE is selling real backlinks placed on real websites with measurable domain rating. That directly feeds traditional Google ranking signals. That link equity has genuine, long-tested SEO value. The price reflects the cost of securing placement on an actual site with real editorial standards.

Rankavi's mentions are not backlinks. They are built for AI answer engines, where citation and brand mention frequency matter more than domain rating does. If your goal is classic organic ranking, FATJOE's DR-based links remain a strong tool. If your goal is being named inside an AI-generated answer, Rankavi is the more direct fit.

## Speed to your first result

FATJOE typically delivers guest posts and niche edits within a set turnaround, often a week or two per package. The process still involves securing space on a real publisher's site.

Rankavi's mentions move through the platform itself, without waiting on a publisher's editorial calendar. Turnaround is generally faster still.

## No sales calls on either side

Both companies get real points here. FATJOE's entire marketplace works without a sales call, letting you browse, filter by DR, and check out directly. Rankavi works the same way, through a dashboard and API, with no gatekeeping conversation required from either vendor.

## Commitment level

FATJOE's marketplace purchases are one-off by default, no ongoing contract required. The $700-plus managed packages likely involve more structured ongoing work.

Rankavi is also fully pay-as-you-go with zero contract, whether you buy one mention or a full 50-credit bulk pack.

## Transparency you can act on

FATJOE's DR-tiered pricing is a real strength. Seeing the price scale directly with domain rating lets you weigh link quality against cost before you buy. That is more transparent than most link vendors.

Rankavi's transparency is simpler but just as direct. It is one fixed price per mention, visible up front, with no tiers to compare.

## Traditional SEO links versus AI-search mentions

FATJOE is squarely a traditional SEO and link building marketplace. It is built around domain rating and backlink profiles that Google's algorithm has rewarded for years.

Rankavi is built specifically for the newer AI-search landscape. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews form answers from mentions and citations rather than link graphs alone. These are genuinely different growth channels, and many brands will eventually want both.

## Scaling your volume

FATJOE scales through its managed packages starting at $700. Or you simply buy more individual links across DR tiers as budget allows.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs that bring the price down to $12 per mention. No separate managed tier is needed to unlock volume pricing.

## Domain rating versus mention frequency

FATJOE's whole model is built around domain rating as the quality signal, and that makes sense for its purpose. A DR50 link genuinely carries more weight in traditional Google ranking calculations than a DR10 link does. FATJOE's pricing scales directly with that reality. If you are optimizing for classic organic rankings, that DR-based approach remains a sound way to spend a link budget.

Rankavi does not compete on domain rating at all, because that is not the signal it is optimizing for. AI answer engines pull from a wider and different set of signals when deciding what to cite. Consistent, frequent brand mentions across many sources can matter more than a handful of high-DR backlinks. Neither approach is wrong. They are answering different questions about how your brand gets found.

## Running both at once

Because FATJOE and Rankavi solve different problems, plenty of brands end up using both rather than choosing one. A backlink budget aimed at FATJOE's DR-tiered marketplace can keep building traditional SEO authority. A separate budget on Rankavi builds AI-search visibility in parallel. Given how low Rankavi's cost per mention is, adding it to a FATJOE budget is a low-risk test. It does not cut into your existing link building spend.

## Checking out on either platform

FATJOE's checkout flow lets you filter by DR band, niche, and turnaround time before you commit. That makes comparison shopping easy within their own marketplace. You see the price, pick the tier, and pay, all without leaving the browser tab.

Rankavi's checkout is similarly direct. You choose how many mentions you want, whether standalone at $15 or bulk at $12 each. Then you complete the purchase through the dashboard or API without any added friction. Both companies deserve credit for making self-serve genuinely self-serve. They do not use the term loosely while gating real pricing behind a call.

## Thinking about total campaign budget

A brand spending $700 on a FATJOE managed package gets a batch of real backlinks with verified domain rating attached. That same $700 buys close to 60 mentions on Rankavi at the bulk rate of $12 each. Neither number proves one vendor beats the other, since the two products are not solving the same problem. It just shows how far apart the two pricing models actually sit once you put real dollars next to them.

## Which one you actually need

FATJOE remains a solid, transparent choice if you need real backlinks with measurable domain rating for classic SEO. If your goal is getting cited by name in AI answers, at a fraction of the cost, Rankavi wins out. Many brands will find room for both in the same strategy.
