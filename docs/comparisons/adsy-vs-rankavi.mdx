## Two Self-Serve Marketplaces, Different Products

Adsy and Rankavi both let you sign up without a salesperson. That is where the similarity mostly ends. Adsy is a guest post and link marketplace. It connects buyers to a network of publishers. Rankavi sells unlinked brand mentions for AI answer engines. Think ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

## Starting Price, Compared Honestly

Adsy deserves credit here. Its pricing is genuinely published. Guest posts start from $14, with per-publisher rates shown before checkout. You know the cost before committing to anything. Rankavi's standard rate is $15 per mention. That drops to $12 with a 50-credit bulk pack. On paper, Adsy's floor price looks slightly lower.

But price per unit only tells part of the story. The two products are not interchangeable. A $14 guest post is a full article on a publisher's site. A $15 Rankavi mention is a targeted brand citation. It is built to surface inside an AI-generated answer. Compare them by purpose, not just sticker price.

## What It Costs to Actually Get Started

Adsy requires a minimum $25 account top-up first. That is on top of whatever the post itself costs. Rankavi has no minimum spend at all. You can buy a single $15 mention if that suits you.

## Locked Into Anything?

Neither company requires a contract. Adsy runs order by order through its marketplace. Rankavi runs pay-as-you-go with no long-term commitment. This is one category where both companies score well. Buyers get real flexibility from each.

## Getting Your First Placement Live

Adsy's marketplace shows publisher turnaround times upfront. Many orders complete within days, depending on the publisher chosen. Rankavi's mentions are generated through its own system. It does not depend on a third-party publisher's schedule. That tends to make first results faster and more predictable.

## Seeing What You Paid For

Adsy shows per-publisher pricing and site metrics before checkout. That is a real strength of the marketplace model. You can inspect domain authority before you buy. Rankavi's dashboard shows cost, placement, and performance per credit. It offers similar visibility, but for a different kind of asset.

## The Real Fork in the Road: What Are You Optimizing For

This question should actually decide your budget. Adsy is built for traditional SEO. It focuses on link equity from guest posts. That approach still works for many sites today. Plenty of sites still need backlinks from diverse domains.

Rankavi is not competing in that lane. AI answer engines don't crawl and rank pages that way. They generate answers from brand mentions across the web. Frequently there is no link involved at all. If you want to show up in ChatGPT answers, a guest post marketplace was never built for that. Rankavi was.

## Scaling Beyond a One-Off Order

Adsy scales the way most marketplaces do. Order by order, publisher by publisher, volume adding up manually. Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and an API. You can request mentions programmatically as needs grow. There is no repeated marketplace browsing each time.

## What Adsy Does Well

Adsy runs a genuinely large publisher network. Its transparent per-listing pricing is a real advantage. Buyers can compare options before spending anything. If your goal is classic backlink diversity, Adsy's model helps. It gives you direct control over which publishers you use. Few competitors in that space offer the same level of choice.

## Running the Numbers on a Real Order

Say you need 50 placements this month. On Adsy, at roughly $14 per post plus the top-up, you would spend at least $725. That excludes any premium publisher fees. Prices climb fast for higher-authority sites. On Rankavi, a 50-credit bulk pack costs $600 flat. There is no separate top-up required. The gap widens further with premium Adsy publishers.

This does not make Adsy a bad deal. Its per-publisher pricing lets you choose exact sites. Rankavi's flat-rate model does not offer that same choice. But for pure cost efficiency, Rankavi's bulk rate is hard to beat.

## Matching the Tool to the Goal

Teams building a diverse backlink profile often lean on Adsy. Its marketplace format suits that kind of link diversity. Teams focused on AI-generated answers tend to choose Rankavi. That is the specific outcome its product targets. Many growth teams end up using both tools together.

## What Each Company Does With Your Order

Adsy routes your order to a publisher from its network. That publisher writes or hosts the content on its own domain. Turnaround depends on that publisher's schedule and workload. Rankavi handles everything internally through its own system. There is no third party involved in fulfillment at all. That structural difference explains much of the speed gap.

It also explains a reporting difference worth noting. Adsy's after-order visibility depends partly on the publisher too. Some publishers report back faster than others. Rankavi's reporting is consistent because one system handles every mention. The dashboard experience does not vary order to order.

## A Few Questions Worth Asking First

Before choosing, ask what you actually need placed. A guest post is a full article on someone else's domain. It is indexed, crawlable, and built to pass link equity. A Rankavi mention is a citation for an AI-generated answer. These solve different visibility problems entirely. Many serious strategies now need both approaches.

Ask also how your buyers are searching today. If most traffic still comes from typed Google queries, Adsy remains sound. If more prospects now ask ChatGPT instead of typing, that traffic never sees a ranked link. Rankavi is built specifically for that shift.

## A Note on Content Quality

Adsy's guest posts pass through a publisher's editorial process. That can mean genuine editorial polish on the final piece. Some publishers review content quality before it goes live. Rankavi's mentions are not editorial content in that same sense. They are structured citations engineered for AI retrieval instead. Judge each asset by what it is actually meant to do.
