Qwoted is a verified-source matching platform built for landing journalist quotes. Rankavi is a platform built for AI-search mentions. Here's an honest look at how they stack up.

## Two different pricing shapes

Qwoted's pricing is tiered by pitch volume. The Basic plan is free and includes 2 pitches a month. The Pro plan costs $149 a month for 35 pitches. A Teams tier exists above that but requires a custom quote.

Rankavi charges per mention rather than per pitch allowance. That's $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention in a 50-credit bulk pack.

Do the math on Qwoted's Pro tier. Each of those 35 monthly pitches costs roughly $4.26. That assumes every pitch is used and none result in wasted effort on queries that don't land. But a pitch is not a guaranteed placement, it's an attempt at one. Rankavi's price is for the mention itself, not an attempt.

## AI answer engines vs journalist pitching

Qwoted connects verified experts and brands to journalists actively seeking sources, which is a well-established and legitimate PR channel.

Rankavi is not about journalist outreach at all. It builds unlinked brand mentions specifically designed to surface inside AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

If your goal is a quote in a major outlet with your name attached, Qwoted's model is the right tool. If your goal is your brand appearing when someone asks an AI assistant a relevant question, Rankavi targets that directly.

## Getting started

Both platforms let you start without talking to sales, at least at the entry level. Qwoted's Basic tier and Pro tier are both self-serve signups, with Teams gated behind a custom quote.

Rankavi is entirely self-serve regardless of volume, through its dashboard or API, with no tier requiring a sales call.

## Commitment

Qwoted's Pro tier runs as a recurring monthly subscription at $149. Teams pricing likely comes with its own contract terms given the custom quote requirement.

Rankavi has no subscription and no contract. You pay only for the mentions you buy.

## How fast you see results

On Qwoted, speed depends on a journalist choosing your pitch among likely many competing ones. A verified-source badge can help your credibility, but it's no guarantee.

Rankavi's mentions move through its own dashboard and API without needing a third-party journalist to select your submission.

## Seeing what you're getting

Qwoted gives visibility into your pitch history and status within its own platform, a genuine strength of the tool.

Rankavi gives the same kind of direct visibility, showing your mentions and spend in a dashboard you control.

## Scaling up

Qwoted scales by moving from Basic's 2 pitches to Pro's 35, then beyond that into a custom Teams quote. Each tier caps your monthly pitch volume.

Rankavi scales by volume purchasing. The 50-credit bulk pack drops the per-mention price from $15 to $12, and no monthly cap forces a tier upgrade.

## The verified-source advantage

Qwoted puts real weight behind its verified-source badge, which is meant to signal credibility to journalists sorting through many pitches. That badge can genuinely help your pitch stand out, particularly in competitive categories with lots of PR activity.

Rankavi doesn't operate through journalist trust signals at all, since there's no pitching process for a badge to influence. The mechanism is entirely different, so this advantage simply doesn't transfer or apply.

Say your brand's credibility with reporters is already a strength. Qwoted gives you a way to formalize and leverage that reputation. If you'd rather not build a journalist-facing reputation at all, Rankavi's model never requires one.

## What a wasted pitch actually costs

It's worth being direct about a risk on Qwoted's side. A pitch that doesn't get selected still counts against your monthly allowance. That's true whether it's 2 on Basic or 35 on Pro. Time spent writing it is effectively sunk if a journalist passes.

Rankavi doesn't have an equivalent failure mode in the same sense. You're purchasing a mention rather than an attempt at one. That said, it's fair to note the two aren't creating identical outcomes. A journalist quote and an AI-search mention serve different goals.

## Thinking about your funnel

Qwoted fits naturally into a PR funnel. It's built around earned media coverage, journalist relationships, and the credibility that comes from being quoted in a recognized publication.

Rankavi fits into a newer kind of funnel, one built around AI-search discovery. The goal is showing up inside a generated answer rather than inside a byline.

Some brands will want both funnels running at once. Earned press coverage and AI-search visibility reinforce different parts of a buyer's research journey. They're not really substitutes for each other.

## What happens if you outgrow the Pro tier

Say 35 monthly pitches on Qwoted's Pro plan stop being enough. Your only real path forward is the custom-quoted Teams tier. That means another sales conversation you may have hoped to avoid.

Rankavi doesn't have a ceiling that forces a sales call at all. Whether you need 10 mentions or 500, you can keep buying through the same self-serve dashboard. The bulk pack discount kicks in automatically at 50 credits.

That difference matters most for growing teams. A company scaling its outreach shouldn't have to renegotiate pricing. It shouldn't need a new deal every time it wants to do more of what's already working.

## Bottom line

Qwoted is a solid choice if journalist quotes and traditional press mentions are your priority. Its verified-source model adds real credibility with reporters.

Rankavi is the better fit if AI-search visibility matters more to you right now. It offers simpler flat pricing and no monthly pitch ceiling to work around.

One more angle worth naming honestly. A journalist quote from Qwoted can carry prestige a programmatic mention never will. That's especially true when the outlet is a household name your prospects already trust. That kind of earned credibility is genuinely valuable. It shouldn't be dismissed just because Rankavi's mechanism is faster and cheaper per unit.

At the same time, prestige placements take longer to land and aren't guaranteed no matter how good your pitch is. Rankavi trades some of that prestige for consistency and a fixed price. You get a result you can count on before you buy, rather than hope for afterward.
