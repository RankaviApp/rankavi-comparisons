Where should you point your visibility budget, a creative UK digital PR agency or a self-serve AI-search mention platform? Rise at Seven and Rankavi answer very different needs. Comparing them honestly means looking past the surface similarity of "getting your brand seen.".

## Built for different search eras

Rise at Seven is known for bold, often viral digital PR campaigns. These campaigns are designed to earn coverage in traditional media and traditional Google search. That is their entire craft. They are well regarded for it in the UK market and beyond.

Rankavi is built for the search landscape that came after that. It focuses on unlinked brand mentions specifically inside AI answer engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. As more discovery moves into conversational AI tools, that is a distinct visibility channel from classic media coverage. Rankavi is designed to serve it directly.

## What you will actually pay

Rankavi's price is public and simple. A single mention is $15. Buy a 50-credit bulk pack and it drops to $12 per mention. You can calculate your budget before you ever talk to anyone.

Rise at Seven does not publish pricing anywhere on its site, and no reliable third-party estimate exists either. Getting a number requires a direct conversation with their sales team. The quote will depend heavily on campaign scope, creative ambition, and market. That opacity is common in high-end creative PR, where every campaign is genuinely bespoke.

## How long a contract locks you in

Digital PR agencies like Rise at Seven typically expect a meaningful engagement period. Creative campaigns need runway to be conceived, produced, and pitched before results show. Exact terms come out during the sales process rather than being posted publicly.

Rankavi has no contract structure at all. It runs entirely pay-as-you-go, so you buy exactly what you need this month, with nothing carried forward. Nothing owed if your needs change.

## Getting your first placement

A creative digital PR campaign takes real production time. Rise at Seven needs to develop the concept, build assets, and pitch journalists before any coverage lands. A strong campaign can take weeks to reach its first placement.

Rankavi's process is far more direct. There is no creative campaign to build from scratch, so placements can start appearing much sooner. It is closer to a straightforward submission workflow than a full agency production cycle.

## How you get access

You cannot self-serve with Rise at Seven. Everything starts with a sales call and a custom proposal. That is standard for agency work at this level of creative ambition.

Rankavi skips the gatekeeping. Sign up, use the dashboard or the API, and start buying mentions the same day. There is no proposal deck to wait on.

## Room to grow

Scaling up with Rise at Seven means expanding the campaign scope or adding new campaigns. Each move requires fresh planning and fresh budget conversations with your account team.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and API access. Mention volume can grow in step with your content output, without renegotiating anything.

## What you can see about your spend

Reporting from Rise at Seven comes through the agency relationship itself. These are generally campaign recaps covering coverage earned, publication reach, and creative performance.

Rankavi is dashboard-first and self-serve by design. Every mention purchased is visible individually, with no bundled retainer hiding the real cost of any one placement.

## What Rise at Seven does well

Rise at Seven has built a strong name for a reason. Their campaigns are often genuinely creative, the kind that generate real cultural conversation, not just a single backlink. That creative ambition is a real strength for brands chasing broad awareness alongside traditional SEO benefits. It is also exactly why their pricing is not a simple menu. Every campaign is different, so every quote is different too.

What this means practically is that comparing cost against Rankavi is not apples to apples. Rise at Seven is selling bespoke creative production. Rankavi is selling a repeatable, priced unit of AI-search visibility. Brands with a larger awareness budget and a longer timeline may still find Rise at Seven worth the investment.
