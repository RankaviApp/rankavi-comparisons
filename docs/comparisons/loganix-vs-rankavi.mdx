## What Each Company Actually Sells

Loganix is a link building service built for agencies and SEOs. It runs guest posts, niche edits, and other backlink placements. Buyers often resell its work under their own brand. Rankavi is different. It builds unlinked brand mentions for AI answer engines. Think ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. One is classic backlink infrastructure. The other is built for how people now search.

## The Pricing Question, Answered Honestly

Here is where the comparison gets uneven fast. Loganix does not publish real pricing on its site. There is a "from $7" teaser. That number is not tied to any confirmed deliverable. To get an actual quote, you book a strategy call. Pricing often depends on volume, niche, and domain rating targets. But you cannot budget confidently before talking to sales.

Rankavi takes the opposite approach. Every mention costs $15 standalone. Buy a 50-credit bulk pack and the price drops to $12. That number is on the site, in the dashboard, and in the API. No call, no negotiation, no guessing what you will owe.

If predictable, published pricing matters to you, this gap is huge.

## Contracts and Commitment Levels

Loganix's contract terms are not published either. Everything routes through a sales conversation first. Wholesale link suppliers sometimes ask for minimum monthly spend. Rankavi has no contract at all. It runs on a pay-as-you-go model. Buy mentions when you need them and stop anytime.

## How Fast You Can Actually Start

With Loganix, your first placement depends on scheduling a call. Then you need a quote and agreed scope. That process can take days before work even begins. Rankavi is self-serve from the first click. You sign up, load credits, and mentions start generating. That happens through the dashboard or API almost immediately.

## Self-Serve Versus Sales-Gated Access

This is really the same story from a different angle. Loganix gates its real pricing behind a strategy call. That suits agencies wanting a dedicated account contact. Rankavi gates nothing. Access is instant and self-serve. It is built for teams who want to test first.

## Transparency Into What You're Actually Buying

Loganix pricing lives behind a call. Transparency depends heavily on who you talk to. Rankavi's dashboard shows exactly what a mention costs. It shows where the mention ran and how it performed. All of that is visible without asking anyone.

## Built for Google Rankings or Built for AI Answers

This is the most important structural difference. Loganix is a traditional link building supplier. Its placements target classic SEO signals like domain authority. That is the kind of backlink profile Google has rewarded for decades. This is still valuable work for many sites.

Rankavi was built for a newer problem. AI answer engines do not rank pages like Google does. They synthesize answers from brand mentions across the web. Often there is no link attached at all. Rankavi's product exists to get your brand mentioned in those sources. If AI-driven discovery matters to your growth, this focus matters a lot.

## Scaling Up Without Growing Your Sales Contact List

Loganix scales through account managers and negotiated volume deals. That works well if you want a human relationship. Rankavi scales through its API and bulk credit packs. A developer or marketer can request more mentions programmatically. No phone call needed at any volume.

## Where Loganix Genuinely Wins

Being fair here matters. Loganix has built real infrastructure for agencies. It has established relationships in the wholesale backlink space. If your priority is traditional link building, that relationship has real value. Loganix's model rewards agencies willing to negotiate. Larger volume commitments can bring per-link costs down. A flat public rate card sometimes cannot match that.

## A Practical Scenario Worth Thinking Through

Imagine you need 20 placements this month. With Loganix, you book a call and describe your niche. You wait for a custom quote before anything starts. Pricing might land anywhere depending on negotiated volume. With Rankavi, you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. That costs roughly $600 for 50 mentions at $12 each. You can start immediately, with the math already visible.

That predictability compounds over a quarter or a year. A marketing team can model Rankavi's cost months out. The rate never hides behind a negotiation. Loganix's wholesale model may still be competitive on a call. Especially at high volume. But you won't know the number until you ask.

## Who Tends to Pick Each One

Agencies reselling backlinks under their own brand often pick Loganix. Wholesale suppliers like this are built for that resale motion. In-house marketing teams chasing AI visibility tend to prefer Rankavi. Its product and pricing are built for direct, fast execution. There is no agency layer in between. Neither preference is wrong. They simply reflect different buying situations.

## What This Means for Different Team Sizes

A solo founder testing brand visibility has very different needs than a large agency. Rankavi's low entry cost suits the solo founder well. You can start with a handful of mentions for under $100. Loganix's wholesale model suits larger operations better. Agencies managing many client accounts benefit from negotiated bulk rates. Neither company built its pricing model by accident. Each reflects a specific kind of buyer.

Small in-house teams often find sales calls frustrating and slow. They want to test a tool without a scheduling back-and-forth. Rankavi's self-serve model removes that friction entirely. Larger agencies, by contrast, often prefer a dedicated contact. That person can adjust scope as client needs shift monthly. Loganix's call-first model fits that relationship style well.

## Which One Fits Your Situation

Figure out what asset you actually need first. A Loganix backlink lives permanently on a chosen site. It passes classic link equity, the signal Google has long rewarded. A Rankavi mention is a citation built for AI-generated answers. No hosting page or link is required at all. These solve different problems. A serious visibility strategy may need both eventually.

If most of your prospects still type queries into Google, Loganix remains a reasonable long-term investment. If a growing share instead ask ChatGPT or Perplexity, that traffic skips ranked search results. That is exactly the gap Rankavi was built to close.

If you need classic backlinks at wholesale scale, and don't mind a sales process, Loganix is a legitimate option. If you want transparent pricing and instant access, Rankavi is the more predictable choice.
