Page One Power and Rankavi solve different problems for different budgets. One is a full-service link building agency. The other is a self-serve platform for AI-search brand mentions. Here is how they actually compare.

## Getting a quote vs seeing a price

Page One Power does not publish pricing anywhere on its site. To find out what a campaign costs, you fill out a lead form and request a "pricing sheet." A sales conversation happens before you ever see a number.

Rankavi skips that step entirely. The price is published: $15 per mention. It drops as low as $12 per mention when you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. You can see the cost before you commit to anything.

This is not a knock on Page One Power. Custom link building campaigns often need scoping calls because sites, niches, and link profiles vary wildly. But it does mean Rankavi is the faster and more transparent starting point if budget certainty matters to you.

## How you actually sign up

Access is one of the biggest differences here. Page One Power routes every prospect through a sales call or gated pricing sheet request. There is no way to just start.

Rankavi is self-serve from the first click. You sign up, use the dashboard or API, and pay as you go. No calls, no gatekeeping, no waiting on a sales rep to get back to you.

If you want to test a small budget this week without a scheduled call, Rankavi is built for that. Page One Power is built for larger, planned engagements with dedicated account management.

## Contracts and commitment

Page One Power's site does not disclose contract terms publicly, which is typical for agencies that sell custom retainers. Expect a conversation about scope and duration as part of the sales process.

Rankavi has no contract at all. It runs on a pay-as-you-go model. You buy what you need, when you need it, and stop whenever you want.

Say a business is unsure how much link building or mention volume it needs long term. That flexibility removes a lot of risk.

## Speed to your first placement

Agency-driven link building takes time. Outreach, relationship building, and manual placement are the core of Page One Power's approach. That process moves at a human pace, often weeks per campaign.

Rankavi is designed to be fast. It runs through a self-serve dashboard and API rather than manual outreach. Placements can move much quicker once you're set up.

Neither speed is wrong. It depends on whether you need a hand-crafted link campaign or fast AI-search mention coverage.

## What each one is actually built for

This is the most important distinction. Page One Power is a link building agency. Its core product is backlinks, earned through outreach and relationship management with site owners and editors.

Rankavi is built for a different goal. It sells unlinked brand mentions designed for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. It is not competing for the same keyword rankings playbook that traditional link building targets.

If your priority is classic SEO authority through backlinks, Page One Power's model is the more familiar fit. If your priority is showing up inside AI-generated answers, Rankavi is purpose-built for that newer objective.

## Reporting and transparency

Page One Power operates as a managed agency. Reporting typically comes through account management, not a public dashboard you can check yourself in real time. That is standard for agency retainers.

Rankavi's self-serve model means you can see your mentions and spend directly. There's no waiting on a status update from an account manager.

## Scaling up

Page One Power scales through more outreach and more account manager hours, an inherently manual process bound by team capacity.

Rankavi scales through its API and bulk credit packs. You increase mention volume without adding manual overhead on either side. The 50-credit bulk pack pricing at $12 per mention specifically rewards higher volume.

## The kind of team behind each result

Page One Power built its reputation on manual outreach. Real link builders research sites, pitch editors, and negotiate placements one at a time. That craftsmanship shows up in the quality of individual links. Clients who value strong editorial fit often stick with agencies like this for years.

Rankavi takes a different path. Instead of a team pitching editors one relationship at a time, it runs a platform. That platform generates AI-search mentions at scale through its dashboard and API. There's no negotiation involved, and no waiting on an individual editor's calendar.

Neither approach is inherently better. A hand-built link from a respected publication can carry weight a programmatic mention never will. But that quality has a cost. It costs you time, price certainty, and your ability to plan a monthly budget in advance.

## Who each option suits best

Page One Power tends to suit established brands with a dedicated SEO budget. It also suits brands with patience for a longer sales and onboarding process. Say you already have six or seven figures earmarked for link building this year. A scoping call is a small cost relative to the campaign ahead.

Smaller teams, or teams testing a new channel for the first time, often find the lead-gen gate frustrating. You cannot try a single placement without first talking to someone. That adds friction before you even know if the fit is right.

Rankavi removes that friction on purpose. Pricing is public and the platform is self-serve. A marketing generalist or a solo founder can test the waters. A handful of mentions is enough before deciding whether to scale up. That lower floor matters if you're still validating whether AI-search visibility belongs in your strategy at all.

## A note on measuring success

With Page One Power, success is typically measured the way agencies have measured link building for years. That means domain authority gained, referring domains added, and keyword rankings that shift over subsequent months. Those are lagging indicators, but they're well understood by most SEO teams.

Rankavi's mentions are aimed at a more immediate, forward-looking signal. Does your brand show up when someone asks an AI system a relevant question right now? That's a newer kind of success metric. It's becoming more important as more searches happen inside chat interfaces instead of traditional search engine results pages.

Say your reporting still centers entirely on classic SEO metrics. Page One Power's output will slot neatly into that existing framework. If you're starting to track AI-search visibility as its own line item, Rankavi is built to feed that specific report.
