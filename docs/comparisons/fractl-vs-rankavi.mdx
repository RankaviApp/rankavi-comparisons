Fractl and Rankavi solve different problems, but marketers often compare them because both promise brand visibility outside your own site. Fractl is a well known digital PR agency. It builds data-driven campaigns designed to earn coverage on major publications. Rankavi is a self-serve platform. It focuses on getting your brand mentioned inside AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Here is how they actually stack up, category by category.

## How you get in the door

With Fractl, you start with a sales call. Their team scopes your goals, proposes a campaign concept, and quotes a custom retainer. There is no public pricing page and no way to self-serve. That is normal for agency-model digital PR, where each campaign is bespoke.

Rankavi skips that step entirely. You sign up, connect your API or dashboard, and start buying mentions immediately. No discovery call, no proposal deck, no waiting on a strategist's calendar. You might want to test the waters with a small batch before committing budget. Rankavi lets you do that on day one.

## What it costs

Rankavi's pricing is simple and public. A single mention costs $15. Buy in bulk, in 50-credit packs, and the price drops to $12 per mention. There are no hidden fees and no retainer minimums.

Fractl does not publish pricing anywhere on its site. Third-party estimates come from agencies and marketers who have worked with them. They cite retainers in the $10,000 to $15,000 per month range. That figure is not confirmed by Fractl itself, so treat it as a ballpark rather than a quote. What we can say with confidence is that Fractl's model is retainer-based, not per-placement. You are paying for the team's time and campaign development. That cost applies regardless of how many mentions land that month.

## Contract terms

Fractl campaigns typically run on a minimum term of six months. That is standard in digital PR, since campaigns need time to build, pitch, and land coverage. You are committing budget before you see results.

Rankavi has no contract at all. It is pay-as-you-go. Buy ten mentions this month and zero next month if that is what your budget calls for. There is no lock-in and no cancellation process to worry about.

## Getting your first result

Digital PR campaigns take real time to plan. Fractl needs to research your industry, build a data story or creative hook, design assets, and pitch journalists. That process commonly takes four to eight weeks before the first placement appears. Results can vary depending on how newsworthy the angle is.

Rankavi placements move faster because there is no campaign to build. You submit what you want mentioned. Mentions can start appearing in AI answer engines much sooner. The platform is built around a repeatable placement workflow rather than one-off pitching.

## Where each one focuses

This is the biggest strategic difference. Fractl's whole discipline is earning links and coverage in traditional media outlets. That is the kind of coverage that ranks well in Google search and builds domain authority over time. That remains valuable for classic SEO.

Rankavi is built specifically for the AI-search era. It targets unlinked brand mentions inside the answer engines millions of people now use instead of a search results page. If your growth channel is increasingly ChatGPT and Perplexity rather than blue links, Rankavi is purpose-built for that shift. Fractl's strength, meanwhile, stays rooted in traditional PR and SEO.

## What Fractl does well

It would be unfair to reduce Fractl to just a price comparison. Their creative team has genuine skill at building data-driven story angles that journalists actually want to cover. A well executed Fractl campaign can land coverage on outlets that self-serve tooling alone will never reach. That kind of earned media still carries real weight for brand credibility and for classic SEO link equity. If your growth plan leans on traditional press coverage, that craft is hard to replace.

The tradeoff is that this craft takes a team, a timeline, and a retainer to access. It is not something you can buy in small, incremental units the way you can with Rankavi. Some brands need AI-search visibility specifically, without a long planning cycle. For them, that difference matters a great deal.

## Frequently asked question

Can you use both Fractl and Rankavi at the same time? Yes, and many brands do exactly that. Fractl can keep building traditional press coverage and backlinks for classic SEO. Rankavi can run in parallel, generating AI-search mentions on its own pay-as-you-go schedule. The two channels do not compete for the same budget line in most marketing plans. One is a long-term retainer and the other is a flexible, per-unit purchase.

## Seeing what you paid for

Fractl reporting comes through the agency relationship, typically monthly recap calls. Campaign reports summarizing coverage earned, domain authority of publications, and campaign performance against goals.

Rankavi gives you dashboard visibility into every mention you have purchased. It is a self-serve platform built around transparent, itemized transactions rather than a bundled retainer.

## Scaling up or down

Fractl scales by adding campaigns or expanding retainer scope. That usually means renegotiating with your account team and committing more monthly budget.

Rankavi scales through the API and bulk credit packs. You can programmatically request more mentions as your catalog or content library grows, without a new sales conversation. That makes Rankavi a better fit for teams who want mention volume to scale with output. There is no negotiated contract in the way.

## Choosing based on your timeline

Budget is not the only factor here. Timeline matters just as much. You might have months to plan a campaign and a story worth pitching. If so, Fractl's process can pay off in coverage that a mention platform cannot replicate. But you might need visibility inside AI answer engines starting this week. In that case, that same process becomes a liability rather than a strength.

Think about where your customers are actually searching. If they still start with a Google query and click through blue links, Fractl's traditional media focus remains directly relevant. They may increasingly ask ChatGPT or Perplexity a question and act on the answer. They may never see a search results page. That is the exact gap Rankavi was built to close. Most brands today sit somewhere between those two extremes, which is why running both channels in parallel is common practice.
