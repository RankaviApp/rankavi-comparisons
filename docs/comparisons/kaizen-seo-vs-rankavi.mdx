Kaizen SEO and Rankavi both publish their pricing. That makes this a rare comparison where you can put real numbers side by side. The bigger difference is what you are actually buying. ## How long you are locked in

Kaizen SEO operates on monthly retainer structures across its content, SEO, and link building tiers. Published pricing gives you clarity, but ongoing SEO and PR work is inherently a longer-term commitment. Rankings and coverage build gradually over months.

Rankavi has no retainer and no lock-in of any kind. It is pay-as-you-go from the first purchase. You decide month to month, or even day to day, how many mentions you want.

## The actual numbers

Kaizen SEO publishes real tiers, which is genuinely rare in this industry and worth crediting. Content packages run from £99 to £399 per month. Full SEO retainers run £1,495 to £3,495 per month. Link building add-ons range from £650 up to £5,000 or more per month, depending on scope.

Rankavi's pricing is also fully published: $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention in 50-credit bulk packs. Comparing the two directly, Kaizen's lowest tier still represents an ongoing monthly commitment for a bundle of deliverables. Rankavi lets you buy exactly the number of mentions you need with no monthly floor.

## What you can verify about spend

Because Kaizen SEO publishes tiered pricing, you get real transparency into their rate card before ever talking to sales. Their reporting presumably follows the same retainer-based structure common to SEO and PR agencies.

Rankavi goes further on granularity. Every single mention purchased is itemized in the dashboard. You always know exactly what each placement cost, not just what tier you are paying into.

## Where each one is built for

Kaizen SEO is a traditional SEO and digital PR agency. Their retainers cover content, technical SEO, and link building. That is the classic toolkit for ranking in Google search and building topical authority over time.

Rankavi is purpose-built for a newer channel. It targets unlinked brand mentions inside AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Your growth strategy may increasingly depend on being cited by AI tools rather than ranking in ten blue links. That is a gap Kaizen's traditional SEO retainers were not designed to fill.

## How fast the first result lands

SEO and content retainers take time by nature. Content needs planning and writing, links need outreach and placement. Rankings need time to settle after any of that work goes live. Even with Kaizen's clear pricing, expect a multi-week runway before meaningful movement.

Rankavi mentions do not require that production cycle. Submissions move through a direct placement workflow. Your first mention can appear far sooner than a typical SEO retainer's first ranking movement.

## Room to scale

Scaling Kaizen SEO generally means moving up a tier. That could mean content packages, full SEO retainers, or larger link building add-ons. Each step raises the monthly cost.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and API access. Buying more volume drops your per-mention price rather than pushing you into a bigger fixed retainer.
