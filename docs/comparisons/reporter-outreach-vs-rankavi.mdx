Reporter Outreach and Rankavi both get your brand mentioned online, but they solve different problems at very different price points. One is a monthly digital PR retainer. The other is a pay-per-mention platform built for AI search. This comparison breaks down both by the numbers.

## Reporter Outreach's published pricing

Reporter Outreach deserves credit for putting real numbers on its site. That is rarer than it should be in this industry. Their tiers are Starter at $3,000 for 7 placements and Growth at $6,000 for 15 placements. Elite runs $12,000 for 32 placements. They also sell à la carte link insertions. Those run $300 for DR50+, $400 for DR60+, and $500 for DR70+ links. That transparency makes it easy to do the math on cost per placement. It lands somewhere between $375 and $430 depending on the tier.

## How Rankavi's price compares

Rankavi charges $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention if you buy a 50-credit bulk pack. Put next to Reporter Outreach's $300 to $500 per link insertion, that is a dramatically different cost structure. Even Reporter Outreach's cheapest full package works out to roughly 25 times more per placement. That is compared to Rankavi's bulk rate. This is not a knock on Reporter Outreach's quality, just a plain comparison of the two price models.

## What you actually get for that money

The gap exists because the two companies are selling different things. Reporter Outreach's placements come from real outreach to journalists and site owners. They aim for links on domains with real domain rating and traffic. That kind of work takes time, relationships, and editorial judgment, and the price reflects that labor.

Rankavi's mentions are built for a different purpose. They show up as a cited brand inside AI-generated answers, not as a backlink on a news site. The two are not interchangeable products even though both involve your brand name appearing somewhere online.

## Commitment and contract terms

Reporter Outreach's packages are billed monthly. They do not publish a stated minimum term. Package-based retainers in this space typically expect at least a few months to show results. You are essentially subscribing to a placement pipeline.

Rankavi has zero contract. You buy mentions as credits and use them when you want. There is no recurring charge unless you choose to buy more. If your budget needs to flex month to month, this matters a lot.

## Getting your first result

With Reporter Outreach, your first placements depend on their outreach team pitching real sites and waiting for replies. Even a well-run agency campaign usually takes a few weeks to land the first confirmed placement. That is because it depends on someone else's editorial calendar.

Rankavi's mentions are not gated by a third party's inbox, so first placements typically move faster. If your launch timeline is tight, that speed difference is worth weighing.

## Self-serve versus package selection

Reporter Outreach's access model is genuinely self-serve. Pricing and packages are posted, and you can pick a tier without a sales call. That is a real point in their favor compared to agencies hiding pricing behind a form.

Rankavi is also fully self-serve, through a dashboard and API. It works at the level of individual mentions, not a monthly package. You are not committing to 7, 15, or 32 placements upfront. You buy what you need.

## Reporting on what you paid for

Reporter Outreach's DR-based link tiers give you a clear quality signal before you buy. The tiers are DR50+, DR60+, or DR70+. That kind of domain rating transparency is useful when you are trying to evaluate link quality against your competitors.

Rankavi's transparency works differently. Every mention has one fixed, published price. You always know your exact cost per unit before you commit any budget.

## SEO links versus AI-search mentions

Reporter Outreach is built around traditional link building and digital PR. It aims to improve your domain rating and organic search rankings. That is still valuable work for classic Google SEO.

Rankavi is purpose-built for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Unlinked brand mentions carry real weight in how those systems form an answer. If your growth strategy leans on AI-search visibility specifically, Rankavi is the more targeted tool.

## Scaling your spend up or down

Reporter Outreach scales by moving up a tier, from Starter to Growth to Elite. Or you add à la carte insertions on top. Each step up is a bigger monthly commitment.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs that drop your per-mention cost to $12. There is no tier structure locking you in. You can scale up for a launch month and scale back down after, without renegotiating anything.

## Thinking about total monthly spend

It helps to look at this from a total budget angle rather than just per-unit cost. A brand spending $6,000 a month on Reporter Outreach's Growth tier gets 15 placements. Each is backed by real outreach to sites with a defined DR floor. That same $6,000 buys 500 mentions on Rankavi at the bulk rate of $12 each. Neither number tells the whole story alone, since the two products serve different goals. But it frames just how differently the two companies price their work.

For a brand building authority through traditional backlinks, Reporter Outreach's 15 placements at vetted domains likely move the needle more. That holds for that specific goal, versus 500 lower-cost mentions. For a brand trying to be named across a high volume of AI-generated answers, the reverse is probably true. Coverage and frequency matter more than any single high-DR link.

## When agencies and platforms work together

These two approaches are not strictly either-or. Some brands run a Reporter Outreach style campaign for backlink authority and organic ranking. Separately, they run Rankavi mentions to build AI-search presence at scale. The budgets do not have to compete with each other if they are solving different problems. What matters is being honest about which problem you are trying to solve before you commit spend to either one.

## Bottom line

Reporter Outreach earns its price with genuine outreach labor and transparent DR-based link tiers. That is more than most agencies offer. But if your goal is AI-search visibility, and you want the lowest cost per mention, Rankavi is hard to match.
