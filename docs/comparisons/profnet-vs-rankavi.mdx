## How you get started

ProfNet, now part of Cision, has run for decades. Journalists post queries there, and PR pros respond with expert sources. There is no signup button that shows you a price. You talk to a sales rep, who prices your account by user count and organization type. That process can take days or weeks before you even see a number.

Rankavi skips that step entirely. You sign up, connect to the dashboard or API, and start ordering placements the same day. There is no gatekeeping call standing between you and a price.

## What each one actually sells

This is the part worth slowing down on, because ProfNet and Rankavi are not quite selling the same thing. ProfNet is a source-matching platform. You get access to journalist queries and you pitch experts into stories. Whether you land a mention depends on whether a reporter picks your pitch, and that is never guaranteed.

Rankavi sells unlinked brand mentions built specifically for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. You are not pitching and hoping. You are ordering a mention placement, and it is priced per unit.

## Price per placement

ProfNet does not publish pricing anywhere on its site. Cision prices ProfNet on a sliding scale tied to user count and organization type. You will not know your real cost until a rep quotes you directly. ProfNet is free for journalists, though. That is part of why the pitching model exists in the first place.

Rankavi's pricing is public and simple. A single mention costs $15. Buy a 50-credit bulk pack and the per-mention price drops to $12. There is no quote, no negotiation, and no waiting on a callback.

## Contract commitment

Cision-owned tools like ProfNet often bundle into annual PR software contracts once you move past a casual free account. Exact terms depend on what your rep sells you, since none of it is posted publicly.

Rankavi runs pay-as-you-go. You buy credits when you need them and there is no annual lock-in of any kind.

## Speed to first placement

With ProfNet, your first outcome depends entirely on a journalist choosing your pitch. That could happen fast, or it could take weeks, or it might never happen for a given query. Response is inherently unpredictable because you are competing against every other PR pro pitching the same reporter.

Rankavi does not depend on being chosen. You order a mention and it gets built into content designed for AI answer engines. The mechanism is direct rather than competitive.

## Access model

ProfNet requires a sales conversation before you see real pricing, and account setup runs through Cision's enterprise sales motion. That suits large PR teams already inside the Cision ecosystem.

Rankavi is self-serve from the first click. No call, no demo, no procurement cycle.

## Transparency and reporting

ProfNet's reporting lives inside the broader Cision suite, and what you see depends on which tier your rep sold you. Since pricing itself is opaque, it is hard to know what reporting comes bundled at which price point.

Rankavi's model is transparent by design. Every mention has a fixed price, so you always know exactly what you paid for what you got.

## AI-search focus vs traditional SEO/PR

ProfNet was built for traditional media relations, connecting sources to journalists for earned coverage in news articles. That is valuable work, but it was not designed with AI answer engines in mind.

Rankavi was built specifically for the AI-search era. Its mentions target how ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews surface brands in answers. That is a different job than ranking a webpage on Google.

## Scalability

Scaling ProfNet means pitching more queries and hoping more of them land, which is manual and unpredictable at volume. There is no bulk-order mechanism for guaranteed placements.

Rankavi scales through its API and bulk credit packs. Buying 50 credits at once drops your cost to $12 per mention, which rewards volume instead of penalizing it.

## Who each tool actually suits

ProfNet still makes sense for large PR teams already embedded in the Cision ecosystem. If your org already pays for Cision's other tools, adding ProfNet can feel like a natural extension. It is not really a new vendor relationship at that point. Teams with dedicated PR staff who pitch daily can get real value from ProfNet's query volume.

Rankavi suits a different kind of buyer. Marketers, founders, and lean teams want a predictable, budgeted way to build AI-search presence. You do not need a dedicated PR person to use it. There is no pitching skill required. You place an order and know your cost before you commit.

## Handling objections before they come up

Some readers will wonder whether a built mention carries the same weight as an earned journalist quote. It is a fair question, and the honest answer is that they serve different purposes. A ProfNet placement can carry the credibility of a named publication and a byline. A Rankavi mention is built to be read and cited by an AI model answering a question. It does not require a human editor's approval.

Both outcomes have value, and neither fully replaces the other. What Rankavi guarantees is a delivered mention at a known price, every time you order one. What ProfNet offers is a chance at earned coverage. There is real upside if you land it, and real risk that you will not.

## What "unpredictable" actually costs you

It is worth naming the real cost of ProfNet's pitch-and-hope model, beyond just time. When your team spends hours crafting pitches that never get picked, that is lost opportunity cost, not just lost hours. Budgeting for PR outcomes gets harder when the same effort might return zero placements one month and five the next.

Rankavi removes that variance from the equation. Every dollar you spend maps directly to a mention. Your AI-search budget behaves like a normal line item instead of a gamble.

## A note on what "expert source" access is worth

To be fair to ProfNet, being quoted as an expert source in a major outlet carries real authority. A built mention cannot fully replicate that. Earned media from a trusted journalist still signals credibility in ways that are hard to manufacture. That is a real strength of the ProfNet model, even with its opaque pricing.

Rankavi is not trying to replace that function. It is solving a narrower, more mechanical problem. It makes sure AI answer engines have accurate, findable mentions of your brand for your category.
