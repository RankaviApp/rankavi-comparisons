Siege Media and Rankavi both help brands get found beyond their own website. They work in different ways and at different price points. Siege Media is an established SEO content and digital PR agency. Rankavi is a self-serve platform built to get brands mentioned inside AI answer engines. Below is a category-by-category look at how they compare.

## What it actually costs

Rankavi charges $15 per mention as a standalone purchase. Buy a 50-credit bulk pack and the price drops to $12 per mention. That is the whole pricing model, published and easy to plan around.

That number is not confirmed directly by Siege Media. It should be treated as an industry estimate rather than a quote. Their pricing is retainer-based, covering strategy, content production, and PR outreach as a bundled service, not a per-placement fee.

## Where the strategic focus sits

Siege Media built its reputation on SEO content married to digital PR. It produces the kind of visual, data-rich content that earns backlinks and ranks in classic Google search. That skill set remains genuinely valuable for organic search performance.

Rankavi takes a narrower, more modern angle. It focuses specifically on unlinked brand mentions inside AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. If AI search is becoming a meaningful discovery channel for your brand, Rankavi is purpose-built for that. A traditional SEO content agency is not.

## Getting started

Working with Siege Media means a sales call first. Their team will want to understand your content goals, current SEO performance, and budget before scoping a retainer. That is standard practice for an agency doing bespoke strategic work.

Rankavi has no sales call at all. You sign up, use the dashboard or API, and start purchasing mentions right away. There is no proposal cycle and no waiting for a kickoff meeting.

## Commitment required

Siege Media retainers commonly run on longer engagement terms. SEO content and PR campaigns need sustained time to compound and show results. You are committing to months of spend before the full impact is visible.

Rankavi carries no contract whatsoever. It is entirely pay-as-you-go, so you can buy exactly the volume you need this month and nothing more.

## How fast results show up

SEO content and digital PR are inherently slow disciplines. Content needs to be researched, written, designed, and published, then PR outreach needs to land coverage around it. Meaningful results from Siege Media typically take months to build momentum, which is expected for organic search growth.

Rankavi mentions move faster because there is no content production cycle involved. You submit what needs mentioning, and placements can begin appearing in AI answer engine responses in a much shorter window.

## Transparency into your spend

With Siege Media, you see performance through the agency relationship. These are typically monthly reports covering content published, rankings achieved, and links earned as part of the retainer.

Rankavi is fully self-serve and dashboard-driven, so every mention purchased is visible and itemized. There is no bundled retainer obscuring what any single placement actually cost.

## Growing your volume

Scaling with Siege Media means expanding your retainer scope. That usually happens through a renegotiation with your account team as your content or PR needs grow.

Rankavi scales through its API and bulk credit packs. You can increase mention volume programmatically as your needs grow, without a new contract negotiation.

If so, Siege Media's content and PR playbook remains a strong fit. Their content is built to rank, and ranking still drives real traffic for a lot of categories.

That behavior sits outside what a traditional SEO content agency measures or optimizes for. Rankavi was built specifically to be visible in that moment. Most brands today see some mix of both behaviors. That is exactly why treating these as separate budget lines, rather than competing ones, tends to work best in practice.
