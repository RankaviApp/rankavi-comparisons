## Let's be honest about the price gap first

Source of Sources, built by HARO founder Peter Shankman, is free. There is no tier, no paywall, and no hidden upsell. Rankavi is a paid service. It starts at $15 per mention and drops to $12 per mention in a 50-credit bulk pack.

If you only look at price, Source of Sources wins outright. It costs nothing, and Rankavi costs money. We are not going to pretend otherwise.

## But they are not solving the same problem

The reason this comparison is not a simple price contest is that the two tools do different jobs. Source of Sources matches journalists with expert sources for their stories, the same category as the original HARO. You pitch, a reporter picks you or does not, and if chosen you get quoted in an article.

Rankavi does not do journalist matching at all. It sells unlinked brand mentions built specifically for AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. You are not pitching anyone. You are ordering a placement directly.

## Access model

Source of Sources runs as a free, self-serve alternative to HARO. It is open to anyone who wants to pitch or post a query. That openness is exactly why it has drawn attention since HARO's own decline.

Rankavi is also self-serve, but it works through direct ordering rather than pitching. You sign up, fund an account, and place orders without waiting on a journalist's decision.

## Speed to first placement

With Source of Sources, your result depends entirely on whether a journalist selects your pitch for their story. That could happen quickly or not at all, since it depends on a human editorial choice you cannot control.

Rankavi's process does not involve anyone choosing you. You order a mention and it enters production for AI answer engines directly, which makes timing far more predictable.

## Price per placement, honestly stated

Source of Sources costs nothing, full stop. That is a genuine advantage for anyone on a tight budget who wants source-pitching access.

Rankavi charges $15 per mention, or $12 per mention at 50-credit bulk volume. That is not free, and we want to be upfront about that rather than dress it up.

## Why the paid option still has a place

Here is the honest case for Rankavi despite the price. Free journalist matching gets you earned coverage if a reporter happens to pick you. It cannot guarantee a placement built for how AI answer engines surface brands. Rankavi's mentions are made for that specific purpose, engineered rather than pitched.

If your goal is being quoted in traditional articles, Source of Sources is a reasonable, free starting point. If your goal is a guaranteed mention aimed at AI search results, that is a different category. Rankavi is built for it.

## Transparency and reporting

Source of Sources works simply: you post or answer a query, and outcomes are visible through whatever the journalist publishes. There is no formal reporting layer since it is a lightweight, free tool.

Rankavi provides a clear, fixed price per mention. Your spend and your output are both easy to track from the start.

## AI-search focus vs traditional SEO/PR

Source of Sources continues HARO's original mission: connecting sources to reporters for traditional earned media coverage. That mission has not changed with the rise of AI answer engines.

Rankavi was built around that exact shift, targeting mentions inside AI-generated answers rather than traditional news articles.

## Scalability

Scaling with Source of Sources means answering more queries and hoping more land. That takes real time with no guaranteed return.

Rankavi scales through bulk credit packs and API access, with cost actually dropping as volume increases.

## Why a free tool cannot cover everything

It is worth asking why a genuinely free tool has not made a paid service like Rankavi obsolete. The answer is that pitching journalists and buying a built mention solve fundamentally different problems. The price gap does not change that. A free tool cannot guarantee an outcome, because the outcome still depends on a reporter's editorial choice.

Rankavi's paid model exists precisely to remove that uncertainty. You are not asking to be chosen. You are commissioning a specific, deliverable mention aimed at a specific target. That target is AI answer engines, not a human editor's inbox.

## What "complementary" really means here

Calling these tools complementary is not just a polite way of avoiding a direct comparison. In practice, a brand could use Source of Sources to build free earned coverage over time. It could also use Rankavi to guarantee baseline visibility inside AI answer engines on its own schedule.

Neither tool makes the other redundant. Free source-pitching and paid mention placement sit at different points in a visibility strategy. Many teams could reasonably run both at once.

## Setting expectations around the free option

To set fair expectations, using a free tool like Source of Sources still takes real time and skill. Writing pitches that stand out, understanding what a reporter wants, and following up appropriately are learned skills. They take practice to do well.

Rankavi requires none of that skill investment. Its simplicity is part of the tradeoff for its price. You pay for the mention, and you do not need to develop pitching expertise to get a result.

## Where Source of Sources came from

Peter Shankman, who built the original HARO, launched Source of Sources partly in response. He watched the space he created shrink and change hands over the years. That background gives it a certain credibility among PR professionals who used the original tool. It carries forward the same free, community-driven spirit HARO started with.

Rankavi has no connection to that lineage and does not claim one. It approaches the same broad category, brand visibility, from a product-first angle. That angle is built for a search landscape HARO never had to consider.

## A quick gut check before you choose

Ask yourself what you actually need this month. If it is press quotes in recognizable publications, start with the free option and see what it returns. You may instead need a guaranteed brand mention an AI model can cite for your category. That needs a paid, engineered mechanism like Rankavi.

Most brands doing serious visibility work will eventually want both tools in their kit, not one instead of the other.
