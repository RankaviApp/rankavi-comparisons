Dig Designs blends digital PR with UX work. That is a slightly different offering than a pure link building or PR shop. Rankavi is a narrower, self-serve tool for AI-search brand mentions. Below is an honest side-by-side look at both.

## Who Dig Designs is built for

Dig Designs combines digital PR campaigns with UX and design thinking. That is a genuinely useful pairing for brands that need both visibility and a better user experience behind the click. That crossover skill set is not common among pure PR agencies. If your project needs someone thinking about how a landing page converts once traffic arrives, that is an advantage.

## No fixed rate card, and what that means for you

Dig Designs does not publish a rate card. Every engagement requires a custom quote, which is typical for agencies doing bespoke creative and PR work together. The tradeoff is that you cannot budget in advance without a conversation first. Pricing likely varies a lot based on project scope, campaign size, and design deliverables involved.

Rankavi sits at the other end of the spectrum. The price is public: $15 per mention standalone, or $12 per mention when purchased in a 50-credit bulk pack. You never need to ask what something costs.

## Getting your first placement or mention

Custom-quoted agency work like Dig Designs typically starts with a scoping call, a proposal, and then execution. That can push your first tangible result out several weeks. That is a normal timeline for creative and PR work that involves real strategy and design.

Rankavi skips that entire process. Mentions are bought and placed through the platform directly, without a proposal cycle. Your first result generally comes much sooner.

## How you get started

With Dig Designs, the entry point is a sales conversation. You describe your project, they scope it, and you get a quote back. There is no way to just sign up and start.

Rankavi's access model is self-serve from the start. You create an account and buy mentions through the dashboard or API. You are running without ever talking to a salesperson.

## Contract length and commitment

Custom-quoted agency engagements in this space often come with a defined project term or a minimum retainer. Dig Designs does not publish specifics, since pricing itself is not published. You would need to confirm this on the sales call.

Rankavi has no contract of any kind. You are buying mentions as needed, and nothing renews or locks you in unless you choose to buy more.

## Seeing what you paid for

A design-and-PR agency like Dig Designs will typically deliver campaign reports and design assets as part of the engagement. That gives you tangible creative output alongside placements. That is valuable if design work is part of what you are paying for.

Rankavi's transparency is simpler. It is a fixed price per mention, visible before you buy, with no ambiguity about what a unit costs.

## Two different goals: brand story versus AI visibility

Dig Designs works in the traditional digital PR and UX space. It aims to build brand stories and improve the user experience that supports them. That is valuable for classic SEO and for conversion once people land on your site.

Rankavi exists for a narrower and more specific purpose. It gets your brand mentioned by name inside AI-generated answers from tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. These systems weigh unlinked mentions differently than a search engine ranks a backlink. Rankavi is built around that mechanic specifically.

## Scaling a campaign versus scaling mentions

Scaling with Dig Designs likely means a bigger scoped project and a new quote. There is no published self-serve tier to move up through.

Scaling with Rankavi is built into the product. Buy a 50-credit bulk pack and your per-mention cost drops to $12, all without any new negotiation.

## Weighing creative value against pure cost

Dig Designs' value rests on the creative and design work layered onto the PR campaign, not just the placements. If you need a redesigned landing page, better conversion flow, or a cohesive visual identity, that expertise has real value. A per-mention product was never meant to replace it. You are paying for strategic thinking and design execution, not just a placement count.

Rankavi is not trying to compete on that front. It has one job. It gets your brand mentioned where AI systems form their answers, at the lowest possible cost per mention. If your project genuinely needs design and UX work alongside PR, Dig Designs' bundled approach may justify its custom pricing. That holds even without a public rate card.

## What the custom quote process typically involves

Expect a discovery call, a written proposal, and some back and forth on scope before a final number. That process protects both sides from a mismatched engagement. Bespoke creative and PR work is genuinely hard to price without understanding the full brief first. It just means you should budget extra calendar time before any campaign or design work actually begins.

Rankavi skips that entire cycle. There is no proposal to review and no scope document to sign off on before you can start. You decide how many mentions you want, pay the published price, and the process begins immediately.

## A practical way to decide

A simple way to think about it is to ask what you are actually buying. If the answer includes design deliverables, landing pages, or a broader creative asset, Dig Designs' custom-scoped model fits that need. If the answer is strictly brand visibility in AI answers, at a known, low cost, Rankavi is the efficient option. Many brands will eventually need both kinds of work. It just may not be from the same vendor at the same time.

## Combining both instead of picking one

Some brands find room for both vendors at once rather than choosing a single winner. A redesigned landing page from Dig Designs can convert better once traffic actually arrives there. Rankavi mentions can drive some of that traffic by getting your brand cited inside AI-generated answers. The two budgets do not compete for the same outcome. Running them together is a reasonable strategy for a brand with room for both line items.

## Which approach makes sense

If your brand needs a combined PR and UX overhaul, and flexibility is no concern, Dig Designs is worth it.
 If your priority is a clear price and showing up in AI-generated answers quickly and cheaply, Rankavi is more direct.
